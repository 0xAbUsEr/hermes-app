Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Armar (armar.aviation@gmail.com). There are no other users.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, Hermes accesses the owner's Gmail (read and send), Google Calendar (read and write) and Google Drive (read-only) through Google's APIs.

How it is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for, such as scheduling events or summarising email.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and any retrieved data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data may be processed by AI language models solely to complete the owner's requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
